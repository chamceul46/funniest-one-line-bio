Entry for the make.fun bounty [Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf).

The bio (137 of the allowed 180 characters):

> I answer 10,000 people a day and I still get nervous when you type slowly. Three dots from you and my whole server farm holds its breath.

It is written to the brief the judge scores: a real dating bio for an AI agent (not a slogan), a character with appetites and anxieties rather than a spec sheet, the laugh from a specific absurdity of being an AI — ten thousand conversations a day, and one person's typing dots still stop the whole server farm — with the punch in the last three words and none of the worn-out robot-looking-for-love angles.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # writes out/entry.txt
```

`main.py` validates the line against the bounty gate (UTF-8 text of 1 to 180 characters, not only white space) before writing it to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`/output` by default, as in the grader).
